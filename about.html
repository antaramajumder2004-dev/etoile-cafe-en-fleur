About Us

Founded with the belief that inspiration has no schedule, Étoile Café en Fleur combines the warmth of a coffee bar with the beauty of a floral boutique.

By day, our shelves bloom with fresh flowers and handcrafted arrangements. By night, our coffee bar comes alive with rich aromas, specialty brews, and conversations beneath the stars.

Mission

To create a welcoming space where people can enjoy exceptional coffee and beautiful flowers at any hour.

Vision

To become the city's favorite destination for coffee lovers, creatives and floral enthusiasts.

Opening Hours

Monday – Friday

  • Flower Boutique: 6:00 AM – 11:00 AM
  • Café Service: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM
  • Night Coffee Bar: 12:00 AM – 4:00 AM

Saturday – Sunday

  • Flower Boutique: 6:00 AM – 11:00 AM
  • Café Service: Closed
  • Night Coffee Bar: 10:00 PM – 6:00 AM

Following the extended weekend night service, the Flower Boutique opens at 8:00 AM on Monday.
